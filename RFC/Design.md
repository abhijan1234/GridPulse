# Requirement : Smart Electricity Grid & Consumption Management Platform

- GridPulse is a platform for an electricity distribution company to manage and monitor its electricity distribution network and customer consumption.

- The system should allow the company to:
    * Manage its electricity distribution infrastructure.
    * Manage customers and their electricity connections.
    * Collect and store electricity consumption data from smart meters.
    * Generate and manage customer bills based on electricity consumption.
    * Monitor the health and utilization of the electricity network.
    * Track outages, failures, and maintenance activities.
    * Provide historical and analytical information about electricity consumption and network performance.
